PSF construction itself is a SLURM job. Submit it from the FASRC tab's "HST pipeline" section (step 2 — Extract HST F814W ePSF). It scans the COSMOS HLSP tiles for bright unsaturated stars and runs photutils.EPSFBuilder; the resulting F814W.fits appears here.
