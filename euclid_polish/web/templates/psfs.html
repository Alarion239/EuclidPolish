Extract empirical ePSFs (FASRC)

Submits a SLURM job that runs photutils' EPSFBuilder on the downloaded cutouts for all four bands (VIS + NISP Y/J/H) in one job. Each band's good stars are K-Means++ clustered by sky position into groups of stars per PSF → one ePSF per cluster (the PSF varies across the field), saved as a multi-extension FITS (HDU0=mean, HDU1..K=cluster PSFs). Generation draws a random convex blend of the K kernels. Bright clipped-core stars are rejected automatically; bands with no cutouts fall back to a Gaussian PSF.
