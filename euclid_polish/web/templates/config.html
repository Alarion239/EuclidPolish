⚙️ Universal job config

Shared knobs used across the pipeline. Set them once here — they persist across reloads/relaunches (~/.euclid_polish/job_config.json) and are sent automatically with the relevant jobs, so the individual pages no longer carry these fields.

Cutouts & PSF

→ /cutouts (download) and /psfs (extract). The PSF clustering knobs (stars per PSF, minimum per cluster) live on the /psfs extract card.

Synthetic scenes & HR size

→ /sky (generate) and /inference (HR size).

Lens-finder dataset

→ the lensfinder-data FASRC step (fewer, bigger fields; own records dir).

Lens-finder training (Zoobot)

→ the lensfinder-train FASRC step (Zoobot fine-tuning). Training is early-stopping-driven: max epochs is just the ceiling.

Star field

→ /sky (synthetic generation): dN/dm ∝ 10α·m over [bright, faint].

Strong lenses

→ /sky (synthetic generation): θ_E from the SIS law, σ_v uniform in [min, max].

Inference display

→ /inference (both reconstruction forms).
