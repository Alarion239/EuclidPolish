Query: brightest N

Sorts mer_catalogue by PSF flux (flux_vis_psf) server-side; magnitudes are proper AB from that µJy flux. Use min mag to skip stars bright enough to saturate (NISP saturates first), min SNR to keep only well-measured flux (flux/σ, e.g. 50) for clean anchors. Runs an integrity tally of any cutouts already on disk afterwards.

Runs as a short SLURM job (so you can cancel it on the FASRC page) and writes stars.csv to the shared netscratch $DATA_DIR the download/PSF jobs read — connect to FASRC first.
