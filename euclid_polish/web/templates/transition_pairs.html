What this page shows

The transition model A_θ (≤5k params) replaces the analytic differential kernel. It learns the map clean ⊛ PSF_HST → clean ⊛ PSF_Euclid — i.e. how to transform an HST-PSF-blurred image into the Euclid-PSF-blurred version of the same scene. Training pairs come from the synthetic clean HR scenes (the existing synthetic pipeline output), convolved with each PSF on the HR grid (0.05″/pix). No noise on either side — the objective is image-formation, not denoising.

The residual view (target − input) shows what the CNN's residual branch literally has to predict. Most of the residual lives near bright sources (where the PSF-broadening difference is most visible); flat sky regions are near zero.
