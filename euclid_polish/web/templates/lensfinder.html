🔎 Lens-finder — does super-resolution surface more lenses?

Replicates the POLISH++ evaluation: finetune a Zoobot binary lens classifier and compare its recovery on LR vs SR vs HR source-centered stamps, reporting TPR-vs-Einstein-radius at a fixed false-positive rate. Run the three FASRC steps below in order; field count / size live in the Config panel (“Lens-finder dataset” section). Stamp / training knobs use sensible defaults (stamp 128 HR px, three LR/SR/HR heads). The final TPR-vs-θE plot is produced locally by scripts/lensfinder_evaluate.py once the heads are synced down.

Pipeline: lensfinder-data (fields → records_lensfinder) → lensfinder-stamps (SR + cut LR/SR/HR stamps) → lensfinder-train (3 Zoobot heads, EuclidPolishZoobot env).
