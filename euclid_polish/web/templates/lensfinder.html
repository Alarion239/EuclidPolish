🔎 Lens-finder — does super-resolution surface more lenses?

Replicates the POLISH++ evaluation: finetune a Zoobot binary lens classifier and compare its recovery on LR vs SR vs HR source-centered stamps, reporting TPR-vs-Einstein-radius at a fixed false-positive rate. Run the four FASRC steps below in order; field count / size live in the Config panel (“Lens-finder dataset” section), and the Zoobot training knobs in “Lens-finder training”. Stamps default to 4-band Lupton-RGB color, cut 53 LR / 106 HR px (424/8, 424/4) and upscaled to 424. The final TPR-vs-θE plot is produced locally by scripts/lensfinder_evaluate.py once the heads are synced down.

Pipeline: lensfinder-data (fields → records_lensfinder) → lensfinder-sr-infer (SR each field → sr_ records, GPU) → lensfinder-stamps (cut 4-band LR/SR/HR color stamps, CPU) → lensfinder-train (3 Zoobot heads, EuclidPolishZoobot env).
