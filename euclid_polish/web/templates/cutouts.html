Download cutouts (FASRC)

Submits a SLURM job that pulls all four bands (VIS + NISP Y/J/H) for every catalog star from the Euclid archive. One shared angular field is sized from the VIS pixel count; each band fetches its own native pixel count.

{% with step_id="download_euclid_cutouts" %}{% include "_step_card_mount.html" %}{% endwith %}

Verify photometry scale (read-only)

Runs verify_star_photometry.py as a short SLURM job (cancellable on the FASRC page): aperture-measures N downloaded VIS cutouts (in electrons) and compares to each star's catalog PSF flux. A median ratio ≈ 1 confirms the absolute electron scale the star-anchor delta-targets use; a constant offset at all radii flags a zeropoint/units bug. Reports only — writes nothing (the ratio table lands in the job log). Connect to FASRC first.

Build star-anchor TFRecords

Turns the downloaded cutouts + catalog PSF flux into the real-Euclid star-anchor training records (records_v2_star_anchor) the training lane consumes. Each usable star → a single-pixel HR delta of its catalog VIS flux at the WCS-exact pixel. --size must match the cutout download.
