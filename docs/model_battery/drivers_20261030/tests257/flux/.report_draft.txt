**Verdict.** On synthetic fields 257@15M keeps faint flux clearly better than 238. It stays within 1.2% of truth in every bin, while 238 is 2.7% short at 24–25 and 4.7% short at 25–26. On real sky the advantage almost disappears. Both models lose the same light against the input (about 2% bright, 5–9% at 23–24.5, about 10% at 24.5–25, pulls near −1.1σ). 257@15M keeps about 1% more only at 23–24.5, and +0.5% overall. A faithful deconvolution should give SR/input > 1 (truth/input is 1.07–1.10 on synthetic), so the real-sky faint deficit is a sim-to-real effect that longer training does not fix.
