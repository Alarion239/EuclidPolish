# Console regrouping and statistics — design

Date: 2026-09-27. Status: grouping and Files placement approved by the user (AskUserQuestion, 2026-09-27); statistics rule revised by the user's answer (below).

Trigger (user): the stat cards are "presented badly, not well readable and not necessary"; Realism › Stars views *colours* and *gaia* are physically meaningless (delete them); the console is "cumbersome and all around the place" — Sky results vs experiments vs catalog eval are unclear, Realism overlaps Data; "don't limit yourself to the past grouping".

Method: a read-only audit of every tab and view (5 auditors + a usage analyst reading the notebook, job log, git history and memory), three independent regroupings (pipeline stages, researcher questions, objects and places), two judges, one synthesis. Raw output: workflow wf_e1f1f4d3-af7.

## Why the old grouping confused

- Results, Experiments and Catalog eval all listed real objects, but each answered a different question, and the console never said which. Results was an inventory: 1,354 tiles from 7 unrelated stores, with a 'production current/stale/missing' bookkeeping column. Experiments was a model-vs-model comparison on tiles you pick, scored with no-truth metrics (holes %, flux R). Catalog eval was the old /evaluation batch run: the production model swept over Q1 lens candidates and galaxies (real, no truth), mixed with synthetic lens and galaxy stamps that have HR truth, plus two synthetic-validation figures. Each had its own run button and its own state words.
- In the new grouping, Sky has three tabs with three distinct questions. Atlas: where is it? Targets: what does production SR look like on each science target, and is it current? The real half of Catalog eval becomes the 'Lens candidates' and 'Q1 galaxies' sets there. Compare: which model is safest on real bright objects? The synthetic stamps and figures leave Sky for Models, because they are synthetic validation.
- Realism was separate from Data because the console grew page by page. Data held the files we downloaded or generated (star list, cutouts, PSFs, TNG, records). Realism held the priors we fitted and the checks against Q1. But every scene ingredient has all three parts: the real reference, the prior fitted to it, and the check. So 'stars' was split across Data › Catalog, Realism › Stars and the atlas, and noise across Realism › Noise, Records and Overview. The new Synthetic workspace is organised by ingredient. Galaxies (with the TNG templates), Stars, Noise, PSF and Fields each hold their real reference, prior and check on one tab. Records holds what comes out, and Status tells you whether you can generate.
- Models holds the whole loop you run after every batch: evaluate, look at curves, train more, refit the gate, promote. The real-holes numbers from Sky › Compare now sit on the Leaderboard and the Combiner, because real data decides promotion. The URL keeps /models/:regime, as /ensemble/:mode does today, so links stay cheap to redirect and two tabs cannot show different regimes.
- Plumbing moved out of the science workspaces: Runs (everything running or run, local and FASRC in one list), Notebook (the lab book and backups) and System (connections, one config editor, lineage, code, storage), with Files as its own rail entry. The Home badge and the Ops badge no longer land on pages that do not show the job they counted.
- The statistics cards were hard to read because the kit printed every value in 22px mono in narrow cells. They were unnecessary because most were sample sizes, constants or totals with false decimals. The replacement shows one sentence per page comparing synthetic against real (or a model against a reference); everything else goes in legends, captions, small tables or collapsed details.
- Every tab name is unique across the rail: Fields is not Compare, Plates is not Targets, and Figures has no 'Results' tab. A label now tells you where you are.

## Decisions

- **Grouping: the Loop console** (chosen over *Objects & places* and *Pipeline stages*, both kept below as alternatives).
- **Files keeps its own rail entry** (`/files`, today's Inspect) — "inspect virtually anything" stays one click away; also reachable from the palette, a top-bar *Open file* icon and every FITS action. The rail has 9 entries.
- **Statistics** (the user's words): *"Make it in a format that is readable and informative. Do not show useless numbers, but show all the necessary information in an accessible and nice way."* See the statistics section — necessary numbers stay visible and readable; only useless ones go.

## Statistics: readable, informative, nothing useless

The cards failed twice: *readability* (a 22 px mono value in a 150 px cell, units and fractions wrapping onto 2–3 lines, the label under the value) and *relevance* (sample sizes, constants, totals with false decimals, duplicates of numbers shown beside them). The replacement keeps every number a researcher needs, in the place and form where it reads best:

1. **Summary line** — where a page has an answer, one body-size sentence above the primary figure states it as a comparison with units and a reference (synthetic vs prior or Q1, gate vs best member, SR vs LR); key numbers bold; the delta warn-toned outside tolerance. At most one per page.
2. **Facts list** — the necessary supporting numbers stay VISIBLE as a readable list: label on the left, value + unit on the right, one row each, body font with tabular numerals, grouped under a plain heading ("Sample", "Fit", "Coverage"). No tiles, no wrapping units. This is where e.g. the Q1 area, the matched-star count and the fit window live.
3. **Comparison table** — whenever two or more things are compared (models, samples, bands), a small table with the reference row first, right-aligned numbers, units in the header.
4. **Counts on the controls that use them** — sample sizes in legend labels, chip counts, segmented options; each count once per screen.
5. **Captions** — normalisation area, release, retrieval date, model version: one muted line under the figure they qualify.
6. **Details** — only provenance-level numbers (fingerprints, hashes, bin counts, tree counts, query configuration, bytes) are collapsed.
7. **Status** — a badge appears only on a problem; the OK state is one quiet line or nothing. Progress counters only inside a running job.
8. **Rounding** — counts are integers; expected/modelled counts ≈3 significant figures (≈403k, never 403,069.7); slopes and scatters 2 significant figures in prose; dB 2 decimals; one unit per quantity everywhere (the Q1 deep-field area is 63.1 deg² everywhere).
9. **Numbers about an image** stay in the viewer readout.
10. **Delete** a number only when it is useless: false precision, duplicates, constants, implementation details, or physically meaningless (the Gaia projection counts).

The kit gains `SummaryLine`, `FactsList`, `ComparisonTable` (or extends DataTable), `Caption` and `Details`; `Stat`, `StatStrip` and `Kpi` leave `ui/display.tsx` once every page has moved (one phase, so styles never mix).

## Alternatives considered

- **Pipeline console (Inputs → Synthetic → Train → Evaluate → Sky → Publish + System)** — Six workspaces in the order of the pipeline stages. Inputs holds the real reference data (PSF chain, TNG, noise table, Q1 samples). Synthetic holds generation, records and the realism checks. Train holds members, curves, submit and combiner. Evaluate holds the leaderboard, synthetic images, diagnostics and a Real benchmark tab (Experiments plus the real half of Catalog eval). Sky holds Atlas and Tiles. Publish holds plates, sheets, crops and the notebook. /settings/config is split up among the stages. *Trade-off:* Its strengths are the most explicit stage map and real and synthetic scoring side by side in Evaluate. But it splits each ingredient again: the Q1 star and galaxy queries go to Inputs while fit and activate stay in Synthetic, which is the Realism/Data overlap under new names. The evaluate → refit → promote loop crosses Evaluate → Train → Evaluate. Dissolving Config loses the one searchable knob editor. Moving the regime to ?regime= and renaming every workspace makes it the most expensive migration, with about 32 tabs.
- **Objects and places (Home, Sky, Scenes, Models, Figures, Runs, Notebook, System)** — Almost the same as this design, but Scenes (Records landing, Compare, Galaxies with TNG templates, Stars, Noise, PSF) has no Status tab, and readiness lives in the Records status line. Train is a view inside Members, Provenance is a view inside System › Files, About sits in Code, and a phased migration is planned. *Trade-off:* It is the leanest (6 Scenes tabs) and cheapest to build; the regime path, the TNG fold and the phased plan are adopted here. But 'Scenes › Compare' collides with 'Sky › Compare'. The most frequent task, submitting a batch every 1–3 days, loses its own tab. Lineage is buried in a file tool. And realism checks land on a data browser rather than on a map of ingredient state.

## Risks

- Build it in phases on top of the in-flight rework. The working tree has uncommitted combiner, spatial_gate, real_field and viewer_data changes, so avoid a big-bang move. (1) The statistics kit plus your deletions: Stars colours/gaia views, the Gaia fields layer, the plate panels, d=axes and RBF. (2) A param-aware redirect table plus the Synthetic merge. (3) The Sky Targets/Compare merge and the Models tab moves. (4) Runs, Notebook and System.
- Redirects must translate query params, not only paths (e.g. ?view=colours, ?view=census, ?view=inputs, ?diag=1, ?g=syn-*, ?view=logs&job=). The Flask matcher reads spa_routes.json paths, so it needs a param-mapping table. The legacy redirects (/evaluation, /inference, /noise, ...) must point straight at the final targets to avoid chains. Palette actions, SkyLinks, figures/model.ts:275-281 'Open in …' links, saved URL keys (?shide=, ?hide=, urlKey np) and tests (realism.test.tsx:497, models.test.ts, workspaces.test.ts, newPages.test.tsx) all change together.
- Deleting the Gaia views must keep the Gaia–Euclid colour sample the stellar prior fits colours on. Remove only the payload keys colors, gaia_cmd and euclid_projection and the plate panels. Switching the colour panels to normalised, forward-noised PDFs is new physics code, so ship it as its own tested step.
- No single staleness service exists yet: Home says 449 stale, Provenance says 0, and producers do not stamp model ids. Until they do, the Loop uses the existing health and state checks, and Lineage says it is incomplete. A wrong 'current' would be worse than today's noise, so the strip shows only actionable rows.
- Merging Sky Results with the real half of Catalog eval joins two backends (real tile stores vs data/eval_results) and two state vocabularies (production_state vs current/stale/unknown/failed). An adapter must map both to current/stale/missing plus 'made by <model>' before the UI can be honest. The lens-candidate groups descend from the deleted lens-finder line, so confirm they remain a science target.
- Models › Images must handle fields and source-centred stamps from three viewer collections ('ensemble', 'evaluation', 'sky'). That is a viewer refactor, not a route change.
- The real-holes columns on Leaderboard, Combiner and Home read Sky › Compare runs (the Combiners.tsx:463-464 ?bench= linkage). They must show the run date and model, or 'no real benchmark for this membership', and never an old number silently.
- Synthetic has 7 tabs, and the real reference data (43k stars, TNG, Q1 samples) sits under 'Synthetic'. This works only if every ingredient tab has the same check → Prior → 'How this is produced' layout and the reference drawer is labelled 'Real reference'.
- Replacing the Stat/Kpi strips touches about 13 files in 6 workspaces. Each headline has to be written by hand, and a half-done migration would leave mixed styles, so replace the kit in one phase.
- (Resolved by the user's choice: Files keeps its rail entry.) Every FITS action, the palette and a top-bar icon still link to it; the tile card needs 'Open in Files' from day one.
- Home thumbnails and the Loop must read caches only. Rendering on open would break the 'opening a page never starts a job' rule and the disk and GPU budget.
- Starless keeps its path param, so its Models tabs remain mostly empty states. The Train button label must state the regime so nobody submits starless members by accident.
