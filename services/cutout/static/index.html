What is this? Euclid Q1 MER images (0.1″ pixels, VIS + NISP Y/J/H) fetched as you pan, and super-resolved to 0.05″ pixels by the POLISH model running on your own GPU (WebGPU) or CPU.

Use: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, drag the divider to compare, keys 1/2/3 switch views, B blinks.

Caveat: the right side is a model reconstruction trained on simulations, not an observation. On real sky it is known to lose some faint flux (~7–10%) and can add faint texture.
